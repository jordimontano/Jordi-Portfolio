# Portfolio design guidance

## Links

- Never add diagonal outbound-link arrows (↗) to links or calls to action anywhere in the portfolio.
- This applies to text characters, SVGs, icon-library equivalents such as `ArrowUpRight`, and CSS-generated decorations.
- Use clear text labels such as “Visit Islero Agency” and “Explore the archive” without a trailing decorative arrow.
- Functional navigation controls, such as Back and gallery Previous/Next, keep their directional icons.
